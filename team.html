Timeshia “Tie” Johnson

Office Manager

Tie is a graduate of Texas College in Tyler, where she earned an Associate of Arts in General Studies and a Bachelor of Science in Biology. She brings her natural organizational skills, along with a strong customer service background, to every Johnson Renovations client.

Throughout your project, Tie will stay in consistent communication with you regarding scheduling, updates, and important details. From start to finish, she’ll be an important ally by your side, helping to make your renovation experience as organized and seamless as possible.

Kay McGrael

Partner + Project Designer

Kay joined Johnson Renovations with a diverse background as an artist and interior designer, as well as a long-standing career with Northwestern Mutual. Her creativity, experience, and eye for design bring a unique perspective to every project.

As you work with Kay, you’ll quickly discover her knack for making design decisions feel easy, enjoyable, and fun. She remains available throughout the project, helping guide you through each step, while naturally building a bond and friendship along the way.
